🐾 Meet Tama! -----------------------

Tama is a tiny desktop cat who lives on your taskbar, your windows, and occasionally wherever the hell she feels like going.

She's still in development, so please poke at things, try to break things, and let me know if Tama does anything particularly weird!

💀 Things to try --------------------

- Pick Tama up and YEET her!
Click and drag Tama to pick her up. Drop her somewhere and she'll fall and land on whatever she can find.

- Put her on your windows.
Tama can walk along the tops of open windows. Try moving, resizing, minimising, or closing windows while she's around.

- Move her between monitors.
If you have multiple monitors, yeet her onto another one and see what she gets up to.

- Let her wander.
Tama walks, sits, sleeps and makes her own decisions. She may occasionally wander completely off-screen. She's fine. Probably.

- Feed her.
Open Tama's pink Tamagotchi UI, select the food icon and press the middle button. Tama will find the food, walk over and eat it.
No touching while she's eating. The noms are serious business.

- Try feeding her from another monitor.
Move the Tamagotchi UI to another screen before spawning food. Tama should make her way over to it.

- Spawn her bed.
The bed is available through the UI too. Its full interaction is still being worked on, but please muck around with it anyway.

- Drag the Tamagotchi UI around.
Objects spawn on the monitor where the UI currently is.

- Close the UI.
The little X closes the Tamagotchi UI without closing Tama herself.
Close and reopen Tama.
Tama has a memory! Her saved state should persist between sessions. Let me know if anything seems to reset when it shouldn't.

🔨 Still being built -----------------

Tama is very much a work in progress. More interactions, needs, animations, jumping/window parkour and other tiny-cat bullshit are coming.

If something breaks, flickers, gets stuck, disappears forever, behaves differently across monitors, or just makes you say “what the fuck are you doing, Tama?” — please tell me what happened and, if possible, what you were doing just before it happened.

🛡️ A note about Windows Security -----

When you first run Tama, Windows may show a security warning because Tama is currently an unsigned, independently made application.

Tama needs to read the positions and sizes of your taskbar and open windows so she knows where she can stand, walk, fall and land.

She does not read the contents of your windows — she only needs information about their position/size and whether they're available for her to stand on.

If Windows shows a “Windows protected your PC” message, you may need to click More info → Run anyway to start Tama.

Most importantly: mess with her.
Half of Tama's job is to be in your way.
Grab the scruff. YEET THE CAT. 🐈‍⬛💨
